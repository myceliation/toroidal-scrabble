How this differs from Scrabble
  • Toroidal board. 15×30 (two boards stacked). Top edge wraps to bottom, left edge wraps to right — words continue across edges.
  • Two centres. Two ★ start squares; the opening move covers one.
  • Doubled bag. 200 tiles instead of 100.
  • Play: tap a rack tile, then a square. Tap a placed tile to recall it. Blanks ask which letter they represent.
  • Legend: TW DW TL DL ★
